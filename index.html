Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Korean Lash Lift? ↓

A specialized technique that lifts and boosts your natural lashes gently, giving a wide-awake look without extensions.

Will lash extensions damage my natural lashes? ↓

When applied correctly — absolutely not. At Glow FX, every single extension is isolated and applied to one natural lash at a time. No clumping, no shortcuts. Lash damage typically comes from poor application, rubbing your eyes, skipping fills, or pulling at extensions. Treat them well and come in regularly, and your natural lashes will stay completely healthy.

I have never had extensions before — where do I start? ↓

Welcome, you are going to love this! Start with a Classic Set — it is the most natural-looking option and a great way to experience extensions for the first time before deciding if you want to go fuller. We will chat at your appointment about length, curl, and style so everything is tailored to you. No guessing, no pressure.
